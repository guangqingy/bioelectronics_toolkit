# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, useful place for researchers,
engineers, students, and maintainers. Contributors are expected to communicate
with care, assume good intent when possible, and make room for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Focus critiques on code, data, methods, documentation, and reproducibility.
- Respect privacy and do not post private data, credentials, or unpublished
  research materials without permission.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, roadmap, and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized attention or unwelcome personal comments.
- Publishing private contact information, data, or credentials.
- Repeated off-topic disruption after maintainers ask for a change in behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
temporarily limit participation; or ban a contributor from project spaces.
Actions should be proportional to the behavior and the safety of the community.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer listed in `CITATION.cff`. Reports
will be handled as privately as the project infrastructure allows.
